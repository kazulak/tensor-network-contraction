### Conclusion to the Research Question
Slicing typically incurs a factor of ~2x more FLOPs for every $1$ order of magnitude ($2^1$) you shave off the peak tensor size (equivalent to halving your memory requirement), provided that you choose the **right indices**. Randomly guessing which indices to slice almost always incurs the $2^k$ multiplier penalty to the computation time while practically saving zero peak memory, because arbitrary indices are rarely part of the central bottleneck contraction.
